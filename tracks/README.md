# Tracks

Each track is one worked example, one checker, and one set of checks.

| Track | What it covers | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [Track 1](track-1-flexure-example.md) | Flexure checks for one concrete beam with carbon fiber fabric, ACI 440.2R-17 Chapter 10 | Result recorded in results/track-1-flexure/. Publisher confirmation of the strengthened strength stays open. Service concrete stress (Section 10.2.8) is not checked, and by hand the beam fails it, about 3.38 ksi against 0.60 f'c = 2.40 ksi; issue #4 item 1. |

Later tracks, in the order Shahid set:

- Track 2: the fire case, and the rest of ACI 440.2R-17 Section 9.2 beyond the Eq. (9.2) strengthening limit check that Track 1 already carries, plus Chapter 9 in full.
- Track 3: shear strengthening with fabric wraps.
- Track 4: a masonry wall case, once a published worked example is found. Masonry sits outside ACI 440.2R, which covers concrete. The masonry guide is ACI 440.7R, and Track 4 will list it in NOTICE when it opens.

A later track opens only when Track 1 has a checker that matches its
example. One beam, checked well, comes before four beams checked loosely.
